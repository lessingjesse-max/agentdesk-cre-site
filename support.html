Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Entirely in your own private iCloud account and on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or any outside server. See our Privacy Policy.

My data isn't syncing between devices.

AgentDesk CRE syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device, that iCloud Drive is enabled, and that the devices have a network connection. Sync can take a moment to catch up.

Why does the app ask for Contacts, Calendar, or Camera access?

Only to power features you choose to use — linking a contact, showing your appointments alongside your deals, or capturing property and tour photos. You can manage these anytime in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records inside the app, or remove everything by deleting the app and clearing its data in your iCloud settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
